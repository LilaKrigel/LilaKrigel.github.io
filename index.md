My research bridges the gap between experimental biology and computational genomics.

## Academic Background

My academic journey reflects a multidisciplinary approach to life sciences and management:
* **MSc in Biology:** Completed my first master's degree in the Ulitsky lab with a primary focus on wet-lab experimental biology.
* **Master's in Business Administration:** Completed an additional master's degree to gain broader strategic, organizational, and analytical perspectives.

## Research Interests & Future Plans

My core focus lies at the intersection of **computational biology** and **RNA regulation**, specifically concerning **long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs)**. 

Currently, our research is dedicated to identifying and characterizing lncRNAs across a diverse range of species. By employing comparative genomics, my goal is to trace the evolutionary trajectories of these transcripts to better understand their origins, conservation, and fundamental biological functions throughout evolution.

## Course Goals

I am currently taking the *Programming in Python using AI* course. My main goal is to improve my AI skills for Python programming so I can write better, more efficient scripts to support my computational research and evolutionary analyses.
